# Design System: Senkaimon Verse

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Senkaimon Verse"**

The site is a passage through a gate and then a room of paper and wood behind it. Black lacquer frames every region, cool washi paper carries the words, dark grained wood holds what is shelved or rolled, and the space beyond the gate is a velvet black lit only by taking darkness away. One vermilion cord runs through the whole system: it is the control at the gate, it becomes the line that experience and technologies hang from, and its colour marks every action a visitor can take. Everything else is held in ghosted greys until it is struck.

Density is low and deliberate. Type is large, sections are tall, and the content holds one thing at a time: on the gate the doors and their verse; on the shelf one pulled-forward book; on the scroll one unrolled sheet. The gate is locked shut until the visitor pulls the cord, pushes down, or follows a menu link, and then a timed opening plays. Motion is heavy and decisive, like weighted wood; ghost-to-struck states snap with no transition. Bleach is evoked through original material (gate, paper, lacquer, cord, hanko seal, ink butterflies), never through borrowed artwork.

All copy and project data in the shipped build is placeholder by design and is marked as such in the interface; nothing here describes real projects, employers, or biography.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Four materials: lacquer frames, washi paper, grained wood, void depth.
- One accent, vermilion, for the cord, controls, and the red ink of the seal and stamps.
- Three type voices: heavy display gothic, Mincho for anything written or spoken on paper, quiet gothic for UI and prose.
- Square surfaces and structural borders; the only rounding is on turned wood (door pulls, scroll rollers) and the focus ring.
- Tactile texture from two tiling SVG grains (paper fibre, wood grain), never from image files or photography.
- Light and focus conveyed by removing darkness, not by adding colour.

## Colors

A near-monochrome lacquer-and-paper palette with warm-brown wood, a single vermilion accent, and a faint violet light.

### Primary
- **Cord Vermilion** (cord): the cord line at the gate and close, the thread running down Experience and Technologies with its diamond knots, the tassel, text selection, scrollbar thumb.
- **Lit Vermilion** (cord-lit): vermilion as it reads on black. Inline action links, struck nav items, focus rings, the chosen book's title, the perched butterfly, caret colour.
- **Deep Vermilion** (cord-deep): vermilion as it reads on paper. The hanko seal disc, stamped fact borders and labels, the close buttons' bottom rule, the resume tag hover.
- **Tassel Knot** (tassel-knot): the darker knot at the head of the tassel, used only inside the tassel drawing for form.

### Secondary
- **Seam Light** (seam): pale violet-white light leaking at the gate seam and flooding the passage; also the faint under-glow beneath book covers. Light only, never a fill for text or controls.

### Neutral
- **Black Lacquer** (lacquer): the frame. Gate lintel and posts, door borders, the fixed bar, the close section, the cover spine, portrait border, text on paper.
- **Lacquer Edge** (lacquer-2): the 8px top beam on lintel and close, hairline rules, the bar border, the technology group plate.
- **Void** (void): the Dangai. Page background, work, thread and About sections, knot rings.
- **Washi** (washi): cool paper, never cream. Doors, covers, strips, the scroll, the close buttons, primary text on black.
- **Bright Washi** (washi-hi): the brightest paper: verse slips, the hanging resume tag, text on vermilion.
- **Ghost** (ghost): dim, unstruck state. Nav items, field labels, pending links, credit line, the rail.
- **Mist** (mist) and **Mist High** (mist-hi): secondary prose on black (summaries, role line, dates, stacks) and organisation names and group plates.
- **Soft Ink** (ink-soft): secondary text on paper (placeholder notes, tag sub-label).
- **Wood, Deep Wood, Pull Wood** (wood, wood-deep, wood-pull): the kumiko lattice bars (wood), shelf planks and scroll rollers (wood-deep), the recessed door pulls (wood-pull). Always carry the wood grain.
- **Paper shades** (paper-shade, paper-shade-edge, paper-shade-side, paper-shade-wash): translucent brown used only to age paper: the inset glow and vignette on doors, side shading on the scroll, the tint of the portrait slot. They darken paper; they never read as a colour of their own.

### Named Rules
**The One Cord Rule.** Vermilion marks the cord and what hangs from it, what can be acted on (controls, links, focus), and the owner's red ink (the hanko seal and the stamped facts). It never decorates headings, fills sections, or tints imagery.

**The Removal of Darkness Rule.** Focus and arrival are shown by lifting a shade (unchosen books sit dimmed to 55% brightness and rise to full when chosen; the passage floods with seam light), not by adding colour or brightness to the focused thing.

**The Cool Paper Rule.** Paper is washi (cool, slightly green-grey). The paper-shade browns only age its edges; never warm the paper itself toward cream or parchment.

## Typography

**Verse Font:** Shippori Mincho B1 (with Yu Mincho, serif), weights 500 and 800
**UI / Body Font:** Zen Kaku Gothic New (with Yu Gothic UI, system-ui), weights 400, 500, 700

**Character:** A blunt, heavy gothic for names, numerals, dates and section titles against a sharp-serifed Mincho for anything written or spoken on paper; a calm gothic handles reading and controls so the two loud voices never compete with the prose. All headline sizes are fluid clamp() values scaled to viewport width; the ramp below is the full set in use.

- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.7, max 62ch): summaries and prose. **Body small** (0.9375rem): stack lines, pending stand-ins. **Action** (700, 1.25rem): close buttons; the contact link uses 1.125rem; **Credit** (0.875rem): the closing credit line.

- **Label** (700, 0.75rem, 0.16em, uppercase): field labels, status and placeholder notes, sub-labels; the technology group plate uses 0.8125rem. **Label small** (0.6875rem, 0.12em): the registry's resume status.
- The seal initials are SVG text at 46 units inside a 120-unit viewBox, in the display face.

### Named Rules
**The Spoken Line Rule.** Mincho is for lines written or voiced on paper (verse slips, cover poems, the About lead, organisation names, technology strips). It never sets UI, labels or buttons.

**The Numbered Registry Rule.** Destinations and volumes carry two-digit display numerals (01, 02...). Numbers are part of the name, set in the display face.

**The Label Is Data Rule.** Uppercase tracked labels name a field, a status, a group or a destination. They never sit above a heading as an eyebrow.

## Layout

Full-bleed bands stacked vertically, side padding of the gutter (clamp(16px, 4.4vw, 72px)). Work content is capped at 1240px; the thread (Experience and Technologies) is capped at 1100px; the About scroll at 1100px. Sections breathe on viewport-relative vertical padding (roughly clamp(96px, 16vh, 180px)).

The gate is a sticky 100svh stage (min 560px) inside a 170svh track when motion is allowed: lacquer lintel on top, then a two-column frame of paper doors between lacquer posts (post width clamp(10px, 3.4vw, 56px)). The left door carries the three-line verse stepping in (second line indented 0.7em, third 1.4em); the right door carries the resume tag hanging from the top and the hanko seal at the foot. The work section is pulled up 70svh over the track so its head arrives with the black beat.

The gate is scroll-locked while closed. The cord, a downward wheel, touch or key push, or an in-page menu link plays the opening (3200ms, scrolling the passage itself), then unlocks; scrolling back to the top locks it again. With reduced motion or without JavaScript the gate stays whole and the page reads straight down.

Projects is a wrapping shelf of 3:4 books (up to 340px wide, three across) with one detail panel (max 820px) under it. Experience and Technologies share a three-column grid: dates (clamp(6.5rem, 15vw, 14rem)), the cord column (3.25rem), then content, so dates sit left of the cord and roles right. About is a 3:7 grid (portrait slot, text) on the scroll paper. The close is full width between two door panels (clamp(36px, 12vw, 200px)). Prose holds to a 62ch measure.

At 760px and below: splits collapse to one column, the shelf becomes a swipeable snap row (books 62% wide), the thread moves to the left edge with dates above roles and the rail hidden, the portrait caps at 200px, the lintel stacks, the registry becomes two columns, the right door puts the tag in its own top band above the seal, and the fixed bar drops the name and numerals to one row of 44px targets. Every interactive element keeps a 44px minimum height.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is material, not floating. Lacquer, paper, wood and void sit in planes: doors slide behind lacquer posts, books stand on planks, paper slips and strips are pasted or hung in front of what they sit on, the scroll hangs between two rollers. Shadows are soft and directional, never hard-offset, and two of them are light rather than shadow (the seam glow and the violet under-glow on covers). Texture is two tiling SVG noise tokens in :root: `--grain-paper` (paper fibre) and `--grain-wood` (stretched wood grain).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lintel drop** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 30px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.65)`): the lintel over the doors.
- **Door edge** (`box-shadow: ±18px 0 40px -18px rgb(0 0 0 / calc(0.7 * var(--door)))`): grows on the inner edge as doors part.
- **Paper age** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 70px rgb(60 45 30 / 0.14)`): the inner glow on the doors.
- **Slip shadow** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 14px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): verse slips on the lattice.
- **Hanging tag** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 18px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`): the resume tag; technology strips use a deeper `0 10px 16px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)` on void.
- **Seam glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 70px 22px rgb(233 227 255 / 0.55)`): light at the gate seam.
- **Cover glow** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -30px rgb(233 227 255 / 0.2)`): faint violet light under book covers on void.
- **Plank** (`box-shadow: 0 20px 28px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.9)`): the wood plank each book stands on. **Roller** (`0 8px 18px -6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)`): scroll rollers.
- **Close button** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 24px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)`): the paper buttons on the close.
- **Seal ink** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 6px 10px rgb(60 20 10 / 0.25))`): the hanko, stamped on paper.

### Named Rules
**The Planes Not Cards Rule.** Elevation comes from one material sitting in front of another (lacquer over paper, paper over void, books on planks). Do not add generic card shadows to lift content off a background.

**The Two Grains Rule.** Paper surfaces take the paper-fibre grain and wooden parts take the wood grain, nothing else is textured. Grain is a tiling SVG token, never an image file, and never laid over text-bearing vermilion or the void.

## Shapes

Square by default. Corners are 0 on every surface and control; softening is limited to the 2px focus ring, the 3px recessed door pulls, and the 13px ends of the scroll rollers (turned wood). Borders are structural lacquer: 8px top beams, 10-18px door frames, a 3px tag and portrait border, 2px stamped-fact borders, 4px close-button bottom rules, 1px hairlines. The kumiko lattice is a coarse 3 by 4 grid of wood bars (clamp(5px, 0.55vw, 9px)) masked over paper. Verse slips and strips are plain rectangles, slightly rotated by up to about 1.2 degrees (strips hang at varied drops of 26-48px). Stamped facts tilt about 1 degree. Timeline knots are 14px squares rotated 45 degrees, 10px on rails. The hanko seal is the one circle, drawn with a roughened edge and tilted -7 degrees. Covers are 3:4 portrait rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
Few, square, and made of paper.
- **Shape:** square corners (0).
- **Primary (close resume):** Washi paper with grain, Black Lacquer text, 700 at 1.25rem, padding 18px 28px, a 4px Deep Vermilion rule on its bottom edge, close-button shadow. Hover turns the text Deep Vermilion. The contact link is the same shape at 1.125rem.
- **Unavailable:** when the resume file is missing, the same shape renders as a non-link with transparent fill, Mist text, and a 2px Lacquer Edge border, with a "Coming soon" sub-label.

### Resume Tag
A paper tag hanging from a 2px lacquer string at the top of the right door; it sways 3 degrees when reached for (motion allowed). Bright Washi, 3px lacquer border, display face at title size with a Soft Ink label sub-line (PDF / Coming soon). Hover turns border and text Deep Vermilion.

### Hanko Seal
An original vermilion disc with an inner ring and the owner's initials in the display face, on a roughened edge and tilted, stamped on the right door above the foot. Decorative only (hidden from assistive tech); it never acts as a control.

### Links
- **Strike links:** Lit Vermilion, 700, 2px underline offset 0.28em; hover flips text to Washi while the underline stays vermilion.
- **Pending stand-ins:** where a link does not exist yet, Ghost text at 0.9375rem says so plainly ("add later"). Never render a dead link.

### Navigation (Registry)
Numbered destinations plus the resume item in a row on the lintel and in the fixed bar. Ghost at rest, 700 uppercase at 0.14em; numerals in the display face. Hover and focus snap the item to Lit Vermilion. The fixed lacquer bar slides down (0.4s ease-out) once the gate has been passed.

### Companion
Three original ink butterflies (26, 20 and 16px) follow the pointer and flap quickly (0.34s) while it moves, drawn inverted against whatever they cross. Perched on a destination they turn Lit Vermilion and slow their wings (1.6s). Where the companion runs it replaces the small per-link guide butterflies.

### Gate and Cord (signature)
Two kumiko paper doors, each with paper fibre, a masked wood-grain lattice, and a recessed pull on the seam edge. Three verse lines on paper slips ink in left to right on the left door. The vermilion cord and tassel hang at the seam with a narrow vertical lacquer label strip; hover or focus drops the tassel 14px and lights it. The opening parts the doors on a weighted cubic curve, floods the seam light, holds a black beat, then reveals the work.

### Shelf and Books (signature)
Projects are 3:4 washi book covers with a lacquer spine, a Mincho poem at the top, the book numeral at the foot, and title and stack beneath, each standing on its own wood plank. The row is one tablist with roving tabindex (arrows, Home and End move and select). The chosen book rises 18px, its title turns Lit Vermilion and is underlined (colour is never the only cue), and the others dim to 55% brightness (80% on hover). One detail panel opens below with title, stack, summary, role and year, and links. Without JavaScript every panel stays open.

### Thread (Experience and Technologies)
The cord continues as a 4px vermilion line that draws itself down as the section scrolls in. Experience: date in the display face, right-aligned left of the cord; a rotated-square knot on the cord; role in the display face and organisation in Mincho to the right. Technologies: each group hangs a lacquer-edge label plate left of the cord and a 2px rail leaving the cord at a knot; names are grained paper strips hung from the rail on 1px strings, at staggered drops and tilts, swinging to rest as they enter view.

### About Scroll (signature)
A washi scroll between two wood rollers (26px tall, round-ended), unrolling from the top as it enters view. Inside: a 3:4 portrait slot with a 3px lacquer border (shows a labelled empty state until a portrait exists), the Mincho lead, and facts as stamped boxes with 2px Deep Vermilion borders and labels, tilted about 1 degree alternately.

### Close
The gate seen from behind: lacquer ground, a wood-and-paper door on each side that slides shut as the page ends, the cord coming down from the top edge and ending in the same tassel, the closing line, the paper buttons, and a hairline-ruled credit line.

### Placeholder Note
A Label-style status marker (Ghost on black, Soft Ink on paper) attached wherever content is not real yet. It is part of the system: placeholders stay visibly placeholders.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** frame every region in lacquer, put words meant to be read slowly on washi paper, and shelve or roll things on grained wood.
- **Do** keep vermilion to the cord, controls, links, focus, and the owner's red ink (seal, stamps); use Lit Vermilion on black and Deep Vermilion on paper.
- **Do** show state by snapping ghost to struck, with no colour transition on hover.
- **Do** show focus or arrival by lifting darkness (dimmed books, seam flood) rather than adding colour, and add a non-colour cue (underline) wherever colour marks the chosen item.
- **Do** number destinations and volumes with two-digit display numerals.
- **Do** apply the paper grain to paper and the wood grain to wood, through the `--grain-paper` and `--grain-wood` tokens.
- **Do** use a fluid clamp() from the documented ramp for any new type size, and add a ramp step here when a genuinely new one is needed.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: the gate stays whole, the page is unlocked, and nothing parts, swings, draws, or unrolls.
- **Do** keep every interactive target at least 44px tall and every placeholder visibly labelled.

### Don't:
- **Don't** warm the paper toward cream or parchment.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent colour or use vermilion on headings or section backgrounds.
- **Don't** round surfaces or controls; corners stay square apart from the 2px focus ring, door pulls, and scroll rollers.
- **Don't** set UI, buttons or labels in Mincho.
- **Don't** place uppercase tracked labels above headings as eyebrows.
- **Don't** use official Bleach artwork, logos, characters or quoted text; gate, cord, seal and butterflies are original drawings.
- **Don't** use image files or photography for texture; grain is the SVG tokens.
- **Don't** use bouncy or elastic easing for state changes; motion is weighted and decisive.
