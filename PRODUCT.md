# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: hiring managers and recruiters** evaluating a software developer. Their job is to decide whether to interview or hire: scan the projects, read the experience, and download the resume.
- **Secondary: prospective freelance clients** deciding whether to commission the developer.

When the two audiences pull in different directions, hiring wins.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of a software developer. It presents their projects, a downloadable resume, an about section with the things visitors should know about them, and their work experience.

Success means a hiring visitor finds the work and the resume quickly and leaves wanting to interview.

## Positioning

A developer portfolio whose identity is heavily inspired by *Bleach*, the series by Tite Kubo. The site is meant to be memorable among generic developer portfolios, and its own build quality is a work sample of the developer's skill.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required content areas: Projects, Resume (downloadable file), About, Experience.
- Built on the existing codebase: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4.
- Undecided: contact method (email, form, or social links), domain and deploy target, and whether projects get their own detail pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Heavily inspired by *Bleach* (Tite Kubo). This is the user's binding direction. How it is interpreted visually is undecided and belongs to new-work.
- Original work only: no official Bleach artwork, logos, character images, screenshots, or copied text. Evoke the series' spirit without using its IP.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The developer's name, project list, resume file, experience, and bio have not been provided.

Until they are, use clearly marked placeholder content and a resume download slot. Never invent employers, dates, project names, metrics, testimonials, or biography facts.

## Product Principles

1. **Hiring first.** The path to projects, experience, and the resume is never blocked or slowed by spectacle.
2. **The site is a work sample.** Its craft, performance, and polish are evidence of the developer's engineering skill.
3. **Homage, not copy.** Capture what makes Bleach feel like Bleach through original work, never borrowed assets.
4. **Truth only.** Placeholders stay visibly placeholders until real content replaces them.
