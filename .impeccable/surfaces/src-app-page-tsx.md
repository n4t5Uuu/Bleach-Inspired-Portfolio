# Homepage

**Scope:** the portfolio homepage (`/`), production-ready and responsive. The Projects, Experience and About routes get sealed-gate stubs so their links resolve; their full builds are later surfaces.

**Visitor mode:** Experience. The site's own craft is the work sample.

**Job:** hiring visitors (recruiters, engineering managers) scan in about 30 seconds: who this is, their role, the resume, then real work. Freelance clients are secondary.

**Proof:** the projects (placeholders until supplied) and the build itself. Every placeholder stays visibly a placeholder.

**Memorable moment:** pulling the vermilion cord or scrolling breaks the gate's doors apart, the screen drops to black for one beat, and Volume 01 lands.

**Unresolved:** the verse copy (placeholder until the user writes it), contact method, resume file, real project content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the Senkaimon. The visitor stands at a gate whose paper doors carry the developer's own three-line verse; scrolling is the passage through it into the Dangai, where the work waits as numbered volumes. It refuses the category default of a greeting headline over a row of project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black lacquer (#0B0B0C) frames every region; washi paper (#F3F3EF, cool, never cream) fills the doors with a shoji lattice; the Dangai beyond is velvet black (#050506), lit only by removing darkness. Vermilion (#D2381E, lit #FF6A4D on black) is the single accent and belongs only to primary controls and the cord. A pale violet-white light leaks at the seam. Dela Gothic One for the name and volume numerals, Shippori Mincho B1 for the verse, Zen Kaku Gothic New for UI and prose. Original ink butterflies, icon-sized.

STORY: The visitor reads a name, a role and a verse, sees the resume tag and four numbered destinations, opens the gate, and meets the work volume by volume before a vermilion line carries them through experience to a close with the resume and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed gate. A black lintel across the top carries the name (largest type on the screen after the verse), the role, and the nav 01 Projects, 02 Experience, 03 About, 04 Resume as dim ghosts that strike vermilion on hover or focus. Two paper doors fill the rest; the verse falls in giant vertical Mincho, one stanza per door. A paper resume tag hangs on the right door. The vermilion cord and tassel hang at the glowing seam and are the primary control: "Open the gate".

FORM: Senkaimon Verse, the user-steered fusion of my grounded candidates 4 (Senkaimon gate) and 1 (tankobon volume poems), raised by the bolder hand: solid black reserved for the decisive beat, light only by removing darkness, one world held at a time with the rest dimmed, ghost destinations struck forward, gravity-ordered verse, and the cord continuing as the experience timeline. seed:39275dc87da1ab8e

Signature interaction: scroll-linked door parting (doors slide apart as the gate section scrolls, the seam light floods, a one-beat black drop, then Volume 01). The cord button scrolls the passage open. Motion grammar: heavy and decisive, doors move like weighted wood, never bouncy; ghost-to-struck states snap. Reduced motion: no parting, the gate stays whole and the page reads straight down.
